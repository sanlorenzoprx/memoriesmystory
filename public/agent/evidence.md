# Memories: My Story — Public Capability Evidence

The public ASC-01 surface supports only claims the product architecture can substantiate.

## Supported claims

Memories: My Story can provide public, deterministic guidance for beginning a family story in English or Spanish, including:

- warm opening questions;
- themed story questions;
- gentle follow-up prompts;
- interview-plan structure;
- prompts for the story behind a photograph;
- a human start URL into the Memories: My Story application.

The prompt capability is deterministic and does not require access to a family archive or a generative model.

## Product boundary

A Living Memory is intended to preserve a photograph together with authentic human voice and story, context, provenance, and family contribution.

ASC-01 itself does not claim that a Memory Story has been preserved. Preservation occurs only through the ordinary product flow and its durable-save rules.

## Claims not made

The public agent surface does not claim to know family facts, reconstruct missing memories, verify genealogy, identify people in photographs, or speak on behalf of the storyteller.

Generated prompts are suggestions, never testimony.
