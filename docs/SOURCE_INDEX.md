## Historical sources retained for traceability

Earlier Product Review documents, amendments, Packet build specifications, and Good Karma sharing materials remain useful evidence of how the product evolved, but they do not override newer approved Foundation decisions.

In particular:

- **Good Karma Share Policy V1 / share-to-unlock is superseded.** Sharing is now voluntary and does not create a free-memory entitlement.
- **The old five-free-story sequence is superseded.** The current commercial demonstration is one complete free Living Memory Magic Moment.
- **The old 30-second free-story assumption is not the current offer target.** Offer V1 targets up to 10 minutes of source voice and transcription for a Living Memory, subject to cost validation before public launch.
- **Memory Story remains implementation compatibility terminology where already embedded in persistence/code.** Living Memory is the canonical customer/domain aggregate.
- **Facebook identity is not required for the current staging acceptance path.** Email and Google identity are the active launch paths; Facebook is prioritized as a social-sharing destination, not forced as an authentication dependency.

## Reconciliation decisions

- **Living Memory** is the canonical unit of value.
- **Muse** is the memory elicitation assistant and remains source-grounded.
- **Memory Circle** is the branded shared-remembrance experience and is broader than remote video chat: same-room, remote, hybrid, and asynchronous extension all preserve the moment of remembering around a photograph.
- **Share Artifact** is the internal boundary for deliberately exporting selected material from a private Living Memory.
- **Moment → Chapter → Life → Family** is the value ladder.
- Commercial pricing/quantity limits are testable hypotheses beneath the Foundation, not permanent product laws.
- The public landing page must teach the Idea and demonstrate the mechanism before revealing paid pricing.
- Research or third-party evidence may support the Idea/mechanism only when accurately attributed; it may not be misrepresented as customer proof for Memories: My Story.
- Customer testimonials, customer counts, outcome claims, permanence guarantees, and scarcity may not be fabricated.
