# Expanded first release, with solo capture as the entry experience

**Date:** 2026-11-13  
**Status:** accepted scope change, explicitly requested by the product owner in this session  
**Supersedes:** the solo-only release boundary in the September 30 sequencing decision and Definition of Done v1.1. Historical receipts remain unchanged.

## Owner direction

The owner asked to integrate archival capture/scans; Our Living Legacy sessions; Chapters, people, roles, invitations and contributions; search/timeline/resurfacing; complete export, integrity and stewardship; reviewed derivatives, distribution and paid plans into the solo release.

These are required capabilities of one expanded first release. They are not optional post-release work. “Solo” describes the simplest entry experience, not the complete release scope. A person can still preserve their first private Living Memory without joining a session, organizing an album, choosing a plan, or sharing.

## Delivery and acceptance

Implement in independently reviewable vertical slices on main-derived branches. Establish a reliable solo foundation internally, then archival capture, shared sessions, family organization/contributions, retrieval, continuity, derivatives/distribution and paid capacity. Export and operational work can proceed earlier where dependencies allow. Internal checkpoints are not permission to launch an incomplete release.

The release is ready only when every required row in `../EXECUTION/EXPANDED_FIRST_RELEASE.md` has matching evidence. Flags may protect development and staging; a disabled or unfinished feature does not satisfy a required release capability. No placeholder screen, empty endpoint or mock provider is acceptance.

The owner approved scope, not prices, legal terms, vendors, or production deployment. Exact paid offers, billing terms and live activation remain unset. Stewardship authorization, contributor retention/withdrawal rights and inheritance terms need explicit policy before operative access. Work can prepare and test technical controls without inventing these decisions. Approved live identity/provider infrastructure and physical devices are required to prove their respective outcomes.

## Invariants and tradeoff

Protect I-01–I-12 (sources, attribution, additive history, portability and continuity), I-13–I-18 (simple, accessible, bilingual first use), I-19–I-26 (private defaults, deliberate sharing and separate authorship), and I-27–I-30 (no share rewards, configurable limits, replaceable infrastructure). No invariant is weakened. Larger scope increases delivery and verification effort; there is no asserted launch date.

## Coordinated updates

Current state, canonical scope, execution README, phase gates, task queue and Definition of Done now point to the expanded release matrix. The Foundation build order continues to describe implementation dependencies; this decision changes which capabilities must be included before the first release, not source authenticity or customer vocabulary.
