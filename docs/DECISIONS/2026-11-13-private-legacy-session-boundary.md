# Private two-person Our Living Legacy boundary

Authority: the owner's 2026-11-13 direction includes two-person in-person and remote Our Living Legacy in MVP v0.5, uses SureDose video interaction as a reference, and explicitly removes Jitsi fallback. This implements the expanded first-release scope without changing locked Foundation promises.

A conversation pins one owned, preserved photograph (original or its separate corrected scan). The owner deliberately creates it and invites one named participant. The invitation grants only that session's projection, never the private archive. Invitations expire, are consumed once, and can be retried for the same request. A joined guest uses a scoped HttpOnly capability; an authenticated guest may additionally bind their own account. A display name is self-attribution, not verified identity or proof that a distinct human operated the device.

Each participant records their own versioned recording decision. Both must be joined and agree before the recording boundary permits capture. Withdrawal invalidates readiness and increments the consent epoch. Host revocation immediately denies subsequent guest metadata/photo requests. Already displayed or downloaded pixels cannot be recalled. This technical consent record is not final legal consent or retention policy approval.

SureDose's configured-provider call/retry/exit pattern is a reference only. Memories inherits neither code nor provider configuration. There is no Jitsi/public-room/third-party fallback. Provider failure must stay in the app with retry/leave. Live remote transport and recording require separately configured authorized infrastructure; this first slice does not claim a working video call.

D1 stores session membership and consent history; existing private R2 stores the pinned photo. No new provider, paid service, generated testimony, automatic publication, or source mutation is introduced. In-person recording/replay and remote media transport follow as separate tested slices. Migration 0010 must precede deploying this code; no deployment is part of verification.
