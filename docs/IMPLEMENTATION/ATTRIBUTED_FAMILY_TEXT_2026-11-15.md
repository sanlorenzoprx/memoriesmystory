# E04a — Independent attributed family text and review

Base: main `9d184044153ab65d504053f81809acb63f2b0ca6`, after PR #43. This is the first E04 slice, not the whole family-contribution feature: contributor voice/photo originals, recording recovery and each author's transcript review remain required next.

## Human outcome

An owner or scoped invited contributor can write their own recollection and choose their attribution. Viewers cannot submit. Different dates and different accounts stay separate, without platform verdicts or AI reconciliation. Submission explicitly permits owner review and possible inclusion for invited family; it does not publish automatically. The view shows localized source-submission time, distinct from the date a person remembers. Each source revision records the technical review-choice version (`family-contribution-review-v1`). A stale choice must be reviewed again. This versioned implementation is not final legal/retention approval.

Only the author can revise their words. Every revision is immutable, attributable, linked to its previous revision and original grant, and retained alongside earlier words. The owner can include or exclude a specific reviewed version, without editing the author's text. An unreviewed later revision does not replace a previously included version. Withdrawal clears family inclusion; later submission must be reviewed anew. Revocation or loss of the author's original scope hides their source from the family assembly while the owner retains private source history for review. The author can retrieve their own history and request withdrawal after revocation, without receiving other archive/media rights. Final operative contributor/retention/deletion policy remains a release gate.

## Persistence, privacy and recovery

Migration 0017 adds contribution records, source revisions and additive events. Memory ownership, author identity, source text/attribution, previous-revision links and agreement versions are immutable. Deleting archive organization does not rewrite source grant identifiers. Whole-memory deletion cascades the associated D1 source history under the existing owner deletion boundary.

Mutations require account authentication, CSRF, explicit current source scope and contributor permission, bounded input, version checks and user/operation-scoped idempotency. The first statement checks permission inside the D1 batch before it can commit its receipt, revision or event. Access revoked during an await therefore creates no source or false durability receipt. Retries preserve original records; changed bodies and stale versions conflict. Owner inclusion checks source authority inside the batch too. Family reads expose only a pinned reviewed version under current scope; hidden revision text and revision activity/version are excluded from unrelated member responses. No R2 objects, primary voice/photo, completion events or entitlements are changed.

The optional bilingual panel appears on completed owner memories and authorized member views. Human content is never translated in place. Unsent text, attribution and retry key stay on the device under an actor/memory-scoped opaque draft key. Reload and language changes retain writing; local-storage failure clearly says to keep the page open. Save confirmation follows a valid durable acknowledgement and an authoritative own-source read matching submitted words/attribution and state. Matching device drafts are cleared after confirmed whole-memory deletion; existing cross-tab deletion notifications suppress re-creating deleted drafts. Other memories and language preferences remain untouched. Earlier words are paginated rather than truncated silently.
