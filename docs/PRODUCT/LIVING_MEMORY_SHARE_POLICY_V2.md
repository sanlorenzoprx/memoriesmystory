# Living Memory Voluntary Share Policy V2

**Status:** approved  
**Date:** 2026-08-11  
**Supersedes:** Good Karma Share Policy V1

## Principle

**Preserve first. Share by choice.**

A Living Memory begins private. Sharing is a deliberate creator action and a natural continuation of how people have always passed photographs and oral history among family and friends.

## No share-to-unlock

Sharing does not unlock another free Living Memory. No referral credit, story credit, or entitlement is granted merely because the person shares, copies a link, selects a provider, or publishes externally.

The product should create enough emotional and human value that a person wants to share the memory itself.

## Share Artifact boundary

External sharing creates a bounded Share Artifact. The creator must be able to preview what is leaving the private Family Archive.

A first production Share Artifact should be capable of containing:

- the selected photograph;
- a selected authentic voice segment;
- clean captions when enabled;
- selected narrator attribution;
- selected story text/caption;
- restrained Memories: My Story attribution;
- a discovery CTA such as “Create your own Living Memory.”

Private metadata, unrelated relationships, private comments, hidden transcript context, and non-selected family data do not leave by default.

## Initial channel priority

1. Facebook — first public social target.
2. WhatsApp — first direct-family messaging target after native device sharing.
3. Instagram — later visual/social expansion.
4. Native share sheet, link, SMS, and email remain useful transport paths.

## Growth loop

The intended bootstrap loop is:

**Living Memory completed → voluntary share → recipient experiences the memory → recipient recognizes that their own photographs have stories → recipient creates a Living Memory.**

Track this through product events rather than reward mechanics.

## Required analytics

At minimum:

- `first_living_memory_completed`;
- `living_memory_share_prompt_viewed`;
- `living_memory_share_started`;
- `living_memory_share_artifact_created`;
- `living_memory_share_opened`;
- `living_memory_referred_visitor`;
- `referred_first_living_memory_started`;
- `referred_first_living_memory_completed`.

## External-platform truth

Once a copy is shared outside the private archive, Memories: My Story cannot guarantee recall of every external copy. The product must communicate that boundary clearly without using fear-based language.
