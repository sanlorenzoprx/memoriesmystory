# Canonical Product Scope

Use this page to prevent implementation ideas from redefining the product.

## Product

**Memories: My Story** is a global, mobile-first, Cloudflare-first web application for preserving photographs, real voices, individual memories, shared family recollections, and a living family legacy.

It is not an Android application.

## Three core paths

1. **Capture Your Memories:** one person captures or chooses a photograph, optionally receives help remembering, records their real voice, confirms who/what/when/where details and truth states, chooses or creates an album, saves, and shares.
2. **Preserve Together:** family or friends remember around one photograph in person; the application captures the conversation and preserves attributed contributions.
3. **Remember Together:** family or friends join at a distance; the photograph remains fixed as the main visual while participants see one another, talk, tag, preserve, and share the completed Memory Story.

## Launch rules that do not drift

- First five Memory Stories are free through share-to-unlock.
- Each free Memory Story currently includes up to 30 seconds of voice.
- Sharing may be private, family-directed, social, or public.
- Direct social sharing and an explicitly public global feed are included in the first release.
- Muse behaves as a warm family companion and stays invisible unless needed.
- Original voice and photograph remain primary and immutable.
- The completion sentence is locked exactly: **“This memory is now part of your family's history.”**
- The application is globally localizable from the beginning, with strong English and Spanish experiences first.
