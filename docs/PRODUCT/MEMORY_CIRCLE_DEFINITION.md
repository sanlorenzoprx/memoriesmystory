# Memory Circle — Canonical Product Definition

**Status:** approved direction for Life and Family offers  
**Date:** 2026-08-11

## Definition

A **Memory Circle** is a shared remembrance experience centered on one photograph or Living Memory, where family or friends can be together **anywhere** and share the moment of telling the photograph's story.

Memory Circle exists because family memory is social. People do not only produce finished stories; they remember through conversation. One person begins, another recognizes someone in the photograph, someone asks what happened next, a sibling remembers the year differently, somebody laughs at a forgotten detail, and another person adds a photograph from the same day.

Memories: My Story should preserve that human moment without reducing it to a generic conference call or flattening it into one AI-polished narrative.

## Experience modes

Memory Circle is one product concept with multiple participation modes.

### Same room

Two or more people gather around one device or shared display and a photograph. The software can capture the natural conversation and attribute contributions when reliable.

### Remote live

Participants join from different locations. The selected photograph remains the visual anchor while people can see and hear one another and remember together.

### Hybrid

Some participants are physically together while others join remotely. The product treats them as one Circle around the same photograph.

### Asynchronous extension

A person who could not attend can be invited later to hear or view the appropriate shared context and add an attributed recollection. The Circle can therefore continue without pretending everyone must be available at the same moment.

## The photograph is the anchor

A Memory Circle is not a gallery meeting and not a video call with a small thumbnail.

The selected photograph should remain visually dominant or persistently accessible throughout the shared remembrance. Participants are present **around the memory**, not the other way around.

A remote layout should generally prioritize:

1. the photograph or Living Memory;
2. the active storyteller and authentic voice;
3. the other participants and their reactions;
4. lightweight controls and Muse assistance.

## Preserve the telling, not only the summary

The product should preserve the source moment of remembrance when participants consent:

- original conversation audio and, where appropriate, video;
- who contributed which recollection;
- questions that caused useful details to surface;
- corrections and clarifications;
- laughter, pauses, emotion, and other human context when captured in the original recording;
- alternate or conflicting recollections;
- new people, places, dates, events, objects, and relationships surfaced during the Circle;
- additional photographs or related Living Memories contributed during or after the gathering.

A transcript, summary, Reel, Chapter, or Life Story may be created later as a derivative. None of those derivatives becomes more authoritative than the original attributed source.

## Muse inside a Memory Circle

Muse should be even more restrained in a Circle than in solo capture.

Muse may:

- surface one useful question when conversation stalls;
- clarify an unidentified person or place;
- notice a potentially unresolved date or relationship;
- suggest a related existing Living Memory;
- help organize the source after the gathering.

Muse should not interrupt natural storytelling, dominate the conversation, manufacture consensus, or turn the Circle into an interview script.

## Consent and privacy

Every participant must understand when audio/video is being preserved and what access they will receive.

A participant's testimony remains attributable to that participant. The archive owner does not gain authority to rewrite another person's recollection as though it were their own.

External sharing remains a separate deliberate action. Joining a Memory Circle does not automatically publish the conversation or the Living Memory.

## Offer placement

Live Memory Circle is part of the **Life** and **Family** paid levels in Offer V1.

Chapter may support basic asynchronous family contribution invitations, but live shared Memory Circle is deliberately reserved for the top two levels because it is a materially richer collaboration and preservation experience.

## Success definition

Memory Circle succeeds when people can say, in effect:

> We were together around that photograph—even though some of us were miles apart—and we preserved not only the story, but the moment we told it together.

That emotional outcome is more important than call duration, participant count, or generic video-chat engagement metrics.
