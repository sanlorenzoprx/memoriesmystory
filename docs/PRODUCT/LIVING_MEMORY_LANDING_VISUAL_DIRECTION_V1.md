# Living Memory Landing Visual Direction V1

**Status:** approved visual concept brief for mockup generation and React implementation  
**Date:** 2026-08-11

## Core visual idea

The page should visually demonstrate a transformation:

**silent photograph → authentic voice → Living Memory → shared family meaning**

The photograph remains the hero object throughout the design. Software UI wraps around the photograph quietly instead of competing with it.

## Design character

Premium but humane. Warm but not nostalgic kitsch. Technically sophisticated without looking like an AI product. Conversion-focused without looking like a direct-response template.

Reference blend:

- Apple Photos: image respect and simplicity;
- Airbnb: interpersonal trust and approachable polish;
- Calm: breathing room and pacing;
- high-quality editorial family archive: typography and emotional seriousness;
- offer architecture: clear hierarchy, repeated action, objection removal, and outcome-first pricing.

## Desktop hero composition

Left ~45%:

- small eyebrow;
- two-line or three-line H1;
- short supporting copy;
- primary solid CTA;
- secondary text/button CTA;
- small private-first trust line.

Right ~55%:

- large portrait or landscape archival-style family photograph on a warm paper card;
- subtle audio playback/waveform attached to the lower edge;
- short transcript excerpt appearing as if spoken;
- two or three context chips, e.g. `Grandpa Luis`, `San Juan`, `Summer 1972`;
- small `Living Memory` status label;
- no device mockup required unless it improves clarity.

## Mobile hero composition

Stack copy first, then the Living Memory card. The photograph should become large enough to create an emotional pause before the next section.

The primary CTA must remain visually dominant. The secondary demo action may be quieter but must remain easy to discover.

## Magic Moment visual behavior

Use a before/after state within the same photograph rather than two unrelated images.

Before:

- photograph;
- subdued label `A photograph`;
- unanswered context.

After user activates the demo:

- voice waveform animates;
- authentic transcript line appears;
- person/place/date chips fade in;
- label becomes `Living Memory`;
- no exploding particles or AI sparkle effects.

The transformation should feel like meaning arriving, not software showing off.

## Memory Circle visual

The selected photograph remains the center or dominant left panel.

Around it:

- storyteller video tile or portrait;
- 2–4 additional participant tiles/portraits;
- one person can be labeled as remote from another city/country only if useful;
- subtle active-speaker treatment;
- tiny recording/consent indicator;
- attributed recollection card such as `Ana remembers: “That was before we moved…”`;
- optional `Muse` prompt at the edge, not center.

The composition should communicate **together around the photograph from anywhere**, not “business meeting.”

## Moment → Chapter → Life → Family visual

Use an expanding archive metaphor:

1. one Living Memory card;
2. a small Chapter stack of related photographs;
3. multiple Chapter stacks connected to one person's timeline;
4. several people/timelines connected as a Family Archive.

Avoid family-tree genealogy aesthetics as the primary metaphor; relationships matter, but Living Memories remain the core unit.

## Memorial belief visual

Do not show a dramatic cemetery hero.

Use a restrained typographic comparison or a quiet split composition:

- left: name, dates, inscription-like typography;
- right: photograph + waveform + voice quote + relationships/context.

Message: preserve more of the life between the dates.

## Pricing visual

Three premium cards with outcomes larger than feature lists:

- Chapter — warm neutral;
- Life — slightly emphasized as the complete one-person preservation path;
- Family — broader archive treatment.

Do not use neon borders, fake badges, or “most popular” unless actual purchase data later supports that label. An editorial label such as `For one person's full story` is acceptable.

## Color and texture direction

Use a warm neutral base, dark ink typography, subtle natural accent, and muted archival tones. Exact tokens are chosen during implementation after accessibility testing.

Texture may resemble fine paper or soft photographic grain at very low intensity. It should disappear from awareness rather than imitate scrapbook paper.

## Typography

Large editorial headline with strong readability. Body typography should remain familiar, accessible, and calm. Avoid highly decorative script fonts for functional text.

## Motion

Motion is reserved for meaning:

- waveform playback;
- transcript reveal;
- context arrival;
- gentle transition from Photograph to Living Memory;
- subtle progression through Moment → Chapter → Life → Family.

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Mockup acceptance

A visual concept is successful if a reviewer can understand, without explanation:

1. this is about photographs and human stories;
2. authentic voice is central;
3. Living Memory is the product mechanism;
4. AI is subordinate, not the hero;
5. family can remember together from anywhere;
6. the page feels private/trustworthy despite supporting sharing;
7. Chapter, Life, and Family are meaningful outcomes rather than storage tiers.
