# Foundation Traceability

Use this map to prevent implementation from becoming disconnected from the mission.

| Product outcome | Foundation reason | Primary invariants | First evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Capture/import photograph | The photograph is the doorway. | I-02, I-06, I-14 | Original survives enhancement and interrupted upload. |
| Record real voice | Voice carries the person. | I-01, I-06 | Original playback is available after completion. |
| Transcribe and describe | Search and discovery help families understand. | I-03, I-04, I-18 | Generated material stays separate and uncertainty is visible. |
| Muse follow-up | Gentle questions help stories emerge. | I-11, I-12 | One relevant question follows listening; silence is allowed. |
| Durable cloud save | Families must trust preservation. | I-07, I-08 | “Saved” appears only after required assets confirm. |
| Albums and Vault | Individual moments accumulate into a life. | I-02, I-09 | Story remains complete through organization and export. |
| Sharing | A preserved story can reconnect a family. | I-16, I-21, I-24 | Audience choice is deliberate; no forced public action; default social copy is editable/optional and contains no automatically inserted private family content. |
| Memory Circles | Families remember together and differently. | I-05, I-17 | Contributions remain attributed and disagreement survives. |
| Export and custodians | Legacy must outlive the product and account. | I-09, I-10 | Human-readable export and custodian path are tested. |
| Multilingual access | Families should not adapt themselves to technology. | I-14, I-15 | Original language is preserved; UI and speech may differ. |
| Just-in-time access and repeat guidance | Technology should disappear until it is useful. | I-11, I-13, I-14 | Permissions are contextual; progress survives interrupted sign-in; guidance repeats, lightens, and remains replayable. |
| First-five-minute completion | The first experience must prove preservation, not merely explain it. | I-01, I-06, I-07, I-08, I-24 | The acceptance contract in `03_USER_EXPERIENCE.md` passes end to end on a representative phone viewport. |
