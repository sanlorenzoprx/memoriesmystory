# Living Memory Offer V1

**Status:** initial commercial offer contract for implementation and testing  
**Date:** 2026-08-11  
**Governing Foundation:** Living Memory doctrine ratified 2026-08-11

## 1. Commercial premise

Memories: My Story is not sold as storage, transcription minutes, an AI memoir generator, or a digital scrapbook.

The customer buys the ability to preserve more of a person than a photograph alone can carry: the original photograph, authentic voice, story, context, family recollections, and provenance assembled as a **Living Memory**.

The public offer must communicate software-first value. AI is integrated to reduce effort and improve organization, retrieval, transcription, and elicitation; AI is never positioned as the author of the human memory.

## 2. Free Magic Moment

Every new customer may create **one complete Living Memory free** before a paid archive purchase.

The free experience exists to demonstrate the mechanism, not to create an artificial referral economy.

Target entitlement:

- 1 Living Memory;
- one source photograph;
- up to 10 minutes of original voice recording;
- transcription of that source voice;
- restrained Muse assistance;
- source-grounded people/place/date/context suggestions when available;
- preservation and playback of the authentic source;
- private-by-default storage under the user's control;
- voluntary sharing through a bounded Share Artifact when the sharing capability is available.

The free experience must reach the canonical event `first_living_memory_completed` before a paid upgrade prompt is treated as the primary next action.

No share is required to keep the first Living Memory. No share earns another free Living Memory.

The 10-minute free target is a product/offer entitlement and must be cost-validated before public launch. If cost evidence requires adjustment, the experience must still provide enough time to create a genuine Magic Moment rather than a crippled demo.

## 4. Chapter — $247

### Outcome

**Preserve a meaningful chapter of someone's life.**

Examples include childhood, military years, a first home, a marriage, a family business, holidays, immigration, a neighborhood, recipes, school years, or a season of family life.

### Core promise

Choose photographs. Tell the stories in the person's real voice. Let Muse help with transcription, context, and organization. Preserve the originals as Living Memories that can be revisited and deliberately shared.

### Objections the offer should remove

- “I don't know where to begin.” → Start with one photograph; Muse can help with the next question.
- “I don't want to write a memoir.” → Speak naturally; transcription is included.
- “I do not remember the exact date.” → Approximate and uncertain facts remain explicit rather than invented.
- “I am not good with technology.” → Photo-first flow; software handles structure behind the scenes.
- “I want this private.” → Private by default.
- “I want my sister to hear this one.” → Share only the selected Living Memory.
- “I don't want AI rewriting Mom.” → The original voice remains canonical.

## 5. Life — $747

### Outcome

**Preserve the stories that made a life.**

Life is for a broader set of Chapters and includes **Memory Circle**, allowing family and friends to share the moment of remembering together around a photograph.

The offer should emphasize that a person's life is not a polished AI biography. It is a source-grounded collection of photographs, real voices, stories, relationships, differing recollections, and Chapters that can later support a Life Story derivative.

## 6. Family — $1,497

### Outcome

**Build a Living Memory Archive the family can keep enriching across people and generations.**

Family expands from one primary storyteller to a multi-person archive with family-level relationships, richer collaboration, Memory Circles, source-grounded Life Stories, and a long-horizon archive model.

The Family offer should sell continuity and connection, not an artificial promise of technological permanence that has not been operationally proven.

## 7. Memory Circle belongs in the top two levels

Live Memory Circle access is included in **Life** and **Family**.

Memory Circle is not generic video chat. It is a remembrance experience centered on one photograph or Living Memory. Participants may be in the same room, in different homes, in different countries, or in a hybrid gathering.

The goal is to **share and preserve the moment of telling the photograph's story**:

- the photograph remains the visual anchor;
- participants can see and hear one another when remote;
- people may react naturally, ask questions, laugh, correct details, identify people, or remember differently;
- source conversation is preserved according to consent;
- contributions remain attributable;
- uncertainty and disagreement are not flattened into false certainty;
- the Living Memory may be enriched after the Circle without replacing its prior source history.

Asynchronous invitations may extend a Memory Circle after the live gathering so someone who could not attend can add their recollection later.

## 8. Comparison frame

Do not position the paid offer primarily against commodity cloud storage or low-cost transcription.

The larger category belief is:

> We have always found ways to remember the people we love. Photographs preserve faces. Traditional memorials preserve names and dates. A Living Memory can preserve more of the life between the dates: the voice, story, context, relationships, and the way a family remembers together.

This framing must remain dignified. Do not disparage graves, funerals, monuments, faith traditions, or grieving families. Do not use mortality fear as artificial urgency.

## 9. Risk reversal

Target concept for validation before publication:

**The First Living Memory Guarantee** — after creating the first paid Living Memory experience, a customer who concludes that the experience is not what was represented may request a refund within a defined period.

Exact refund terms require operational and legal approval before publication.

Separate non-financial trust promise:

> The original photograph and authentic human voice are never silently replaced by an AI-generated version.

## 10. Offer truth rules

Do not advertise a capability as shipped until the implementation and acceptance evidence exist.

Allowed before customer testimonials exist:

- product demonstration proof;
- clearly attributed research supporting the Idea or mechanism premise;
- technical acceptance evidence for implemented behavior;
- transparent statements such as “designed to” or “coming to Life/Family” where appropriate.

Never fabricate:

- customer testimonials;
- family counts;
- memories preserved;
- permanence guarantees;
- storage duration;
- certifications;
- customer outcomes;
- scarcity or urgency.

Real permissioned customer proof should progressively replace borrowed premise proof after launch.
