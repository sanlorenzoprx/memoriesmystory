# Living Memory Anti-Drift Checklist

**Status:** required product/implementation gate  
**Date:** 2026-08-11

Complete this for every material feature, architecture change, AI capability, growth mechanic, pricing change, or new surface.

## 1. Idea

- What part of the core belief does this strengthen?
- Does it preserve the truth that a photograph can outlive the story that gives it meaning?

## 2. Mechanism

- How does this help create, enrich, preserve, connect, rediscover, or responsibly share a Living Memory?
- Does it make the memory more alive, complete, connected, durable, trustworthy, or retrievable?

## 3. Human source

- Are original photograph/audio/testimony still canonical?
- Are generated derivatives visibly separate?
- Can uncertainty and disagreement remain visible?

## 4. Muse / AI

- Is AI helping a person remember, organize, retrieve, connect, translate, or present source-grounded material?
- Could any output be mistaken for human testimony?
- Does every factual generated claim have a traceable source or explicit uncertainty state?

## 5. Privacy and ownership

- Is the Living Memory private by default?
- Does any boundary crossing require deliberate creator action?
- If shared externally, is a bounded Share Artifact used?
- Is the creator shown what leaves the archive?
- Is the user being pressured or rewarded to publish? If yes, stop.

## 6. Durability and continuity

- What happens offline or during interruption?
- Are originals immutable and durably confirmed before “saved” language?
- Can the family export and understand the record later?
- Does this create future stewardship or migration debt?

## 7. Experience

- Does the feature keep the photograph/person/story central?
- Does it add unnecessary forms, dashboards, settings, AI terminology, or metadata work?
- Is it usable by older adults, lower-powered devices, mixed-language families, and unreliable connections?

## 8. Growth

- Does growth arise from genuine customer value?
- Does sharing preserve dignity and creator control?
- Does attribution explain the mechanism rather than turn the memory into an advertisement?

## 9. Architecture

- Is the new abstraction required by a human/product outcome?
- Are we preserving validated Memory Story/media/truth/receipt infrastructure where it still works?
- Are we changing technology because it improves the product rather than because a new tool is fashionable?

## 10. Decision
